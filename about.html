GNS Event Rentals is an event and party rental company serving weddings, Nikkahs and Walimat-ul-Nikkahs, birthdays, anniversaries, corporate events and cultural celebrations.

Our primary service area is Washington, DC and Northern Virginia. We also serve Dallas–Fort Worth, Texas through a separate market-specific quote process.

Whether you’re planning an intimate celebration or a larger gathering, start with the pieces you love. We’ll review your event requirements, confirm availability and help you understand delivery and setup options.
